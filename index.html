My Top 10 Films

  1. Portrait of a Lady on Fire

    2019 · Céline Sciamma

    A painter and her subject fall in love, told almost entirely in glances.

  2. Spirited Away

    2001 · Hayao Miyazaki

    A child's dream logic at its most generous and strange.

  3. In the Mood for Love

    2000 · Wong Kar-wai

    Two neighbours, one impossible longing, and the most beautiful hallway in cinema.

  4. Parasite

    2019 · Bong Joon-ho

    Every genre at once, and every genre earning its place.

  5. Ikiru

    1952 · Akira Kurosawa

    A bureaucrat learning to live by learning what one useful thing can mean.

  6. The Grand Budapest Hotel

    2014 · Wes Anderson

    Every frame a composition, every composition a joke, every joke a small grief.

  7. Moonlight

    2016 · Barry Jenkins

    Three actors, one boy, and a tenderness Hollywood rarely allows.

  8. Tampopo

    1985 · Jûzô Itami

    A film about noodles that's really about the absurdity of caring about anything.

  9. Arrival

    2016 · Denis Villeneuve

    Language changes what you can imagine, and grief is a second language.

  10. The Red Balloon

    1956 · Albert Lamorisse

    Thirty-four minutes of a boy and a balloon, and more feeling than most trilogies.
